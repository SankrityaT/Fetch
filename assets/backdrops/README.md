Drop backdrop images here (jpg, jpeg, png or webp) and they appear in the editor's
Look panel automatically. No code change needed.

Guidance:
- 2560x1440 is the size the set ships at. Images are scaled to cover the frame and
  centre-cropped.
- Keep the middle calm: the recording sits on top of it with an inset, so busy
  centres fight the content.
- The filename becomes the label, so `night-sky.jpg` shows as "Night Sky".
- A photograph that is not ours gets an entry in `credits.json` (photographer, their
  Unsplash username, the photo's page) and a row in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.
  `test/unsplash.test.js` fails if either is missing.

## The set that ships

Ten photographs from Unsplash, under the Unsplash License, each credited in
`credits.json`, which the backdrop list reads so the picker can name the photographer:

| Warm | Cool | Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| `amber-dusk` light, `pastel-dusk` light, `red-dune` dark | `blue-dunes` light, `forest-fog` light, `crescent-moon` mid, `mountain-haze` dark, `night-sky` dark | `white-plaster` light, `still-sea` dark |

All are 2560x1440 JPEG, about 3MB together. The smooth skies are encoded at a high
quality because a sky bands first; the textured ones at a lower one because their own
grain hides the compression.

Two drawn grounds stay: `linen-bone` and `blueprint-grid`, generated by
`tools/make-backdrops.js`, since a woven surface and a technical grid are not things a
calm photograph gives. The three drawn gradients the photographs replaced
(`warm-dune`, `cold-harbour`, `deep-space`) are gone from here; a look saved with one
still resolves, to `amber-dusk`, `still-sea` and `night-sky` (processor.js,
`RETIRED_BACKDROPS`).

`PROMPTS.md` is the brief the drawn set was designed against, and its one rule (a quiet
middle) is still the rule for anything added here, however it is made.
