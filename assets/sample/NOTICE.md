# The sample library

Everything in this folder was made by Fetch itself, for Fetch, and is covered by
Fetch's own license, GPL-3.0-or-later (see `LICENSE` at the root of the repository).

- `pantry-recipe.mp4`, `pantry-phone.mp4` and `pantry-card.png` were drawn from
  `make/scene.html` by `make/make.js`, one frame at a time, in a hidden window, and
  encoded with the bundled FFmpeg (see `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`). No screen was
  recorded to make them. Run `npx electron assets/sample/make/make.js` to draw them again.
- The sound on both takes is a click on every press over a very quiet room, written
  as numbers by `make.js`. Nobody's voice and no recorded sound is in it.
- The type in the pictures is Geist and Bricolage Grotesque, from `assets/fonts/`,
  both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows them to be drawn into
  pictures and video.
- The `.pointer.json` and `.fetchdoc.json` files are the sidecars a real take carries:
  where the agent's pointer went, and where a phone's glass sits in its frame.

Biscuit's Pantry is made up. It is not a real product and no real person, product or
device is named or drawn anywhere in the sample. The phone is a plain shape called
"Sample phone".
