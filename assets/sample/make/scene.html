Lemon and herb pasta

20 minServes 2Vegetarian

Ingredients

Spaghetti200 g Lemon, zest and juice1 Parsley, choppeda handful Parmesan, grated40 g Garlic, sliced2 cloves

Method

1Cook the spaghetti in well salted water until it still has a little bite.
2Warm the garlic in olive oil, then add the lemon zest and juice.
3Toss the pasta through with a splash of its water, the parmesan and the parsley.
